Log / About

Operations-informed, systems-minded.

As a spacecraft controller at ISRO's Master Control Facility, I'm part of the team operating India's geostationary fleet — monitoring real-time telemetry across every subsystem, executing time-tagged and real-time commanding, and responding when a satellite misbehaves.

My path ran through IIST (B.Tech, Avionics — ECE), where I built an SU-8 MEMS piezoelectric energy harvester and implemented an FMCW radar on software-defined radio. What that mix gave me is a systems-level view: I care less about any single subsystem than about how the whole spacecraft behaves — especially when it misbehaves.

Currently deepening my toolkit in spacecraft communication, payloads, and Python-based operations automation. Always glad to connect with people building India's space ecosystem.
